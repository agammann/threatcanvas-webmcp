# Demo video checklist

- Public YouTube watch URL, not an editor or Studio URL
- Runtime under 3:00
- Audible narration and verified audio stream
- 1080p or clearly legible interface
- Shows the live deployed application
- Shows WebMCP-ready status and registered tool catalog
- Demonstrates at least one read and one shared visible mutation
- Demonstrates the F-104 human-lock refusal
- Demonstrates a five-day capacity-aware sprint
- Explains why WebMCP is better than click simulation
- Contains no private data, credentials, browser notifications, or draft placeholders
- Title, description, visibility, runtime, and audio independently verified on the public watch page
